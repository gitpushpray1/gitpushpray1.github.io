Count down to what's next.

Paydays, cycles and big days, on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Download on the App Store

Free for iPhone. iOS 18 or later.

Countdowns that keep going

Most countdown apps stop when the day arrives. Nigh rolls over to the next one, so the things that come around again are always counted.

💸

Paydays and bills

Every other Friday, rent on the 1st, trash day every Tuesday. Set it once and it keeps counting.

🌸

Cycles with phases

A menstrual cycle or a 21/7 pill pack, with a day-by-day ring and "3 days until the next phase."

🎂

Birthdays and big days

Anniversaries, trips, concerts and weddings. Count in days, weeks, months or "sleeps."

📱

Widgets for everything

Small, medium and large Home Screen widgets, plus Lock Screen widgets. Tap one to open its live countdown.

🎨

10 ways to see it

Big number, progress bar, ring, fill, dots, weekends left, calendar, milestones, live timer and phases.

🔔

Reminders your way

"Payday is tomorrow," milestones at 100 days and 1 week, or custom schedules like the third Friday of the month.

See it in action

Every event gets its own color, emoji and style.

Private by design

Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

  • No account and no sign-up
  • No cloud and no servers. Your events never leave your device.
  • No subscriptions and no in-app purchases, ever
  • Supported by a small banner ad, never in widgets or notifications
